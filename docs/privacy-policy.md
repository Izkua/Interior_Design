## The short version

- You can use the app without an account. Your rooms, furniture, layouts, and photos stay on your phone.
- If you create an account, we store your email, name (if your sign-in provider shares it), your shopping preferences, and a count of AI pictures you've made.
- Catalog searches go through our server to a product-data provider. They don't include your rooms, furniture, or photos.
- AI pictures (when available) send your room's layout, and a room photo only if you add one, to an image-generation provider.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

## What stays on your phone

Rooms, wall measurements, doors and windows, furniture, layouts, wishlist items, and the photos you add to furniture are saved only on your device. When the app suggests the type of a piece of furniture from a photo, that's done by Apple's on-device image recognition; the photo is not uploaded. Room scanning on iPhones with LiDAR uses Apple's RoomPlan, which also runs on the device.

Deleting the app deletes this data. In v1 it is not synced or backed up by us.

## Affiliate links

Some links to retailers may earn us a commission if you buy something. This doesn't change the price you pay. The app shows this notice next to shopping links.

## Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only to take photos of your furniture and room, and for "add photos" access only to save AI pictures you choose to save. Choosing an existing photo uses Apple's photo picker, which doesn't give the app access to your library.

## Your choices

- **Use the app as a guest.** Everything except AI pictures works without an account.
- **Delete your account** in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, preferences, and usage history from our server. Your rooms and furniture on your phone stay.
- **Contact us** at [support email] with any question or request.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Description
Thinking about rearranging, or buying a new sofa? Plan it on your phone first.

DRAW YOUR ROOM
• Type the width and length, or tap in corners for L-shaped rooms and angled walls
• Add doors, windows, and outlets where they really are
• "Lock angles" keeps corners square; the app tells you if your measurements don't add up
• On iPhones with LiDAR, scan the room with the camera (beta)

ADD YOUR FURNITURE
• Pick a type and it starts at a realistic size you can adjust
• Or take a photo: the app suggests what it is and helps estimate its size, which you can always edit

SEE LAYOUTS THAT WORK
• Several arrangements of your actual furniture, drawn to scale
• Every one keeps doors able to open and leaves a clear path to walk
• Drag pieces to adjust, and see what moves and what's missing

SHOP WHAT FITS
• Set a budget and your style
• Every product with a listed size is checked against the free space in your room
• Save favorites and preview them in your layout

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your rooms, furniture, and photos stay on your phone. No account needed.

Some links to retailers may earn a commission at no cost to you. AI pictures are inspiration only; real furniture and placement may differ.
